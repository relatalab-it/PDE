# Story Brief — How large is the market for a public-data service, in Italy and in Europe

## Journalistic angle
Analisi di mercato (the user's wording). The analysis measures how many organisations
operate in the sectors that could use a natural-language service for public data, in Italy
and in Europe, by sector and by size class. It also states the limits of year and coverage of the official figures. It is a market-size analysis, not a forecast of revenues.

## Editorial hook
No specific event or public debate. Structural relevance: measuring the size of a market is
a first step for anyone building or assessing a service, and the figure is rarely stated in
a verifiable way. The user's answer: "è analisi di mercato".

## Primary audience
Policy-aware reader (reader who follows public policy and markets). The narrative register
implies that "active enterprises" and "size class" are understood, while differences between
counting methods are explained in the text.

## Scope
### In scope
- Number of active enterprises in the sectors of publishing of newspapers and periodicals,
  news agencies, management consultancy and market research.
- Italy and the European Union (EU27).
- The latest year available for each source, stated explicitly even where the years differ.
- Breakdown by size class of the enterprise.
- A statement of the year and coverage limits of the available figures.

### Out of scope
- Turnover, spending or revenue of these enterprises, and any forecast of revenues.
- Enterprises in other sectors, and individual consumers.
- How many of these organisations would buy a service: the analysis measures the
  population, not the demand.
- Regions or provinces below national level.
- Causal claims about market growth.

## Output language
Italian and English, with the bilingual toggle. Instruction given by the user on
22 October 2026 (constitution, Article 11).

## Null hypothesis

### Falsifiability condition
This story does not hold if the data show that the official sources do not give, for Italy and for the EU27, a non-confidential number of active enterprises for at least three of the five sector codes of the scope, with the same sector definition and a stated year.

### Methodological floor
For the story to be published: (1) at least three of the five sector codes have a published, non-confidential count for Italy and for the EU27, with the reference year stated; (2) for Italy, the two sources can be compared on at least three of those codes (same code, stated years); (3) the counts are available by size class for at least one of the sources. Values flagged by the source as low reliability or estimated are reported with the flag and are not hidden.

### Scope trigger
Scoped down: if a floor condition fails, the report covers only the codes for which the data exist, and states which codes, countries or breakdowns are missing and why. The report is not reframed to fit the data.

## Clarifications

### Geographic scope
Italy, Germany, France, Spain and the EU27 total. Proposed by Claude, approved by the user on 22 October 2026.

### Time period
The latest year available for each source, with the year stated explicitly for every figure and the difference between years declared in the text. Proposed by Claude (option 1 of 2: latest year per source, not a common year), accepted by the user on 22 October 2026 ("ok").

### Comparison baseline
Two comparisons. Main theme: for Italy, the national statistical office figure against the European statistical office figure for the same sectors (whether the two can be added or compared). Context: the weight of Italy, Germany, France and Spain on the EU27 total, by sector. Proposed by Claude, approved by the user on 22 October 2026 ("ok").

### Unit of analysis
Absolute values (number of enterprises, by sector and size class) as the main measure; Y-axis starts at zero. Shares (weight of a country on the EU27 total, share of enterprises with employees) as contextual reading. No index (no time series). Proposed by Claude, approved by the user on 22 October 2026 ("ok").

### Audience calibration
Explain inline: what counts as an "active enterprise" in each source; what "size class" means (by number of persons employed); why two official sources can give different figures for the same sector. Percentages and shares are used without definition. Proposed by Claude, approved by the user on 22 October 2026 ("ok").

### Comparison level (conditional C2)
Comparisons between countries (Italy, Germany, France, Spain), between sources (for Italy) and between sectors. They may coexist in the report, but each chart shows one level at a time. Formulated by Claude on the user's delegation (22 October 2026, "fai tu").

### Regional breakdown (conditional C3)
Italy at national level only. Regions and provinces are out of scope (see Scope). Formulated by Claude on the user's delegation (22 October 2026, "fai tu").

Conditional questions C1 (structural break) and C4 (policy claim) are not triggered: there is no time series and no policy is evaluated.
