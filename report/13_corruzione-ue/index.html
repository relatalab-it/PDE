A higher number of recorded offences does NOT necessarily indicate more corruption. It reflects the system's detection and prosecution capacity. Slovakia (0.90), Cyprus (1.14) and Ireland (0.06) record very little despite the phenomenon existing. The chart measures institutional responsiveness, not actual corruption incidence.

Come leggere:How to read: L'indicatore misura la percezione dei cittadini sull'indipendenza del sistema giudiziario da influenze politiche e di interesse economico. A justice system perceived as dependent facilitates impunity and discourages corruption reporting.
