Unemployment rate across European countries

% of labour force · annual · 2024 (latest available) · 35 countries

How to read: The unemployment rate measures the share of active workers (employed + job-seeking) who cannot find work. A high rate signals a slack labour market; a low rate signals tight conditions. Values below 4% are generally considered full employment.

Labour force participation: who is in the game?

% of working-age population (15–64) · annual · 2024 · 36 countries

What is the LFPR? The Labour Force Participation Rate measures the share of the population aged 15–64 that is either employed or actively seeking work. A lower rate means more people have dropped out of the job market entirely — not counted as unemployed.

Job security: temporary contracts and involuntary part-time

% of total employees · annual · 2024 (latest) · 36 countries

Precarious work indicators: Temporary employment measures workers on fixed-term contracts. Involuntary part-time measures workers who want more hours but cannot get them — a proxy for underemployment and demand-side weakness.

Labour demand: job vacancy rates by sector

% of total posts (filled + vacant) · EU27 · 2024 annual average

What the vacancy rate signals: A high vacancy rate in a sector means employers are struggling to fill positions — indicating either skill shortages, low wage attractiveness, or structural mismatches. A rising vacancy rate across all sectors is a leading indicator of labour market tightening.
